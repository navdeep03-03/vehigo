Buyer questions

Clear answers for an early competitor site.

Is Vehigo a government website?

No. Vehigo is positioned as a private compliance-support technology platform.

What does the site capture?

Demo requests are saved locally in the browser and can be exported as a CSV for follow-up.

Can this become a full SaaS?

Yes. The next layer would add authentication, database-backed vehicles, payment gateway flows, and partner APIs.
